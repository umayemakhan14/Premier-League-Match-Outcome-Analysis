# ⚽ Premier League Match Outcome Analysis

## 📖 The Story Behind This Project

Every football match leaves behind a trail of data—goals, shots, fouls, cards, and results. But hidden within those numbers are patterns that explain why some teams dominate, why home teams often perform better, and what factors influence match outcomes.

In this project, I stepped into the role of a Data Analyst and explored **9,380 Premier League matches** using **PostgreSQL**. My goal was simple: transform raw football data into meaningful insights.

---

## 🎯 Questions I Explored

- Does playing at home increase the chances of winning?
- Which teams perform best over time?
- How common are draws compared to wins?
- What are the highest-scoring matches?
- What trends can be discovered from historical match data?
